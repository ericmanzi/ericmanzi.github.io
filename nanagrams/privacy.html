Privacy Policy

Nanagrams for iPhone · Last updated October 7, 2026

Nanagrams has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information.

On your iPhone

Your solo game, your best time, and the room code and board of an online game you might rejoin are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online games

When you play online, the app connects to the game server to run the match. It sends the room code, your letter tiles, how many tiles are in your hand, how many words you've made, and, when the game ends, your finished board so your opponent can see it. None of this identifies you.

The server keeps each game only as long as it's needed to play it: game records are set to expire two hours after they're created and are then deleted automatically. The server is hosted on Amazon Web Services, which, like any internet service, sees your device's IP address in order to connect. Nanagrams does not store it.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, apart from your opponent seeing your game during and at the end of a match, and Amazon Web Services hosting the server.

Children

Nanagrams doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
