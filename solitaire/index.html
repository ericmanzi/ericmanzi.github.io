How it plays

1

Open a category

Cards wearing a crown — Greek 0/3, Robots 0/6 — are category cards. Drag one into a crowned slot and it starts collecting. Only four run at a time.

2

Send words home

Drag a word onto its slot. Kappa belongs to Greek, Toronto to Cities — a slot takes nothing else.

3

Stack in the columns

Words of one category stack on each other in the tableau and then travel as a group. A crowned card dropped on its own words caps the stack, and nothing goes on top of a lid. Uncovered cards flip for free.

4

Deal when stuck

Tap the deck as often as you like. Cards you cannot use pile up beside it, top card in play. When the deck runs out, one more tap turns the pile back into it. The last levels deal 64 cards against 128 moves.

When you get stuck

Three hints, five undos and one joker per level — the allowance refills every time a board is dealt.
