How it plays

1

Read the foundation

Each foundation card shows a category — Greece, Robot, Chicago — and how many words it still needs.

2

Send words home

Tap a face-up word in the tableau to move it onto the foundation it belongs to. Flip whatever it was covering.

3

Work the stock

Stuck? Draw from the stock for new words. Every draw and every placement costs one of your moves.

4

Clear the board

Fill every foundation before the move counter hits zero. Crowned foundations pay out bonus coins.
